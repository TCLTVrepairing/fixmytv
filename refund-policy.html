Refund Policy

Last updated: 21 June 2026

This Refund & Cancellation Policy explains how cancellations and refunds are handled for Fix My TV services.

Cancellation

You may cancel a service request before the technician visit by calling or messaging us. If the technician has already reached the location, visit/inspection charges may apply.

Inspection Charges

Inspection or doorstep visit charges are generally non-refundable once the technician has visited and checked the TV.

Repair Refunds

If a paid repair fails within the covered warranty period due to the same repaired part/fault, we will inspect and provide re-service as per warranty terms. Refund decisions depend on the case, part condition and service record.

No Refund Cases

  • Customer rejects repair after inspection
  • Issue is caused by physical damage, liquid damage, voltage fluctuation or misuse
  • Fault is different from the original repaired fault
  • Customer provides incorrect address or is unavailable during visit
